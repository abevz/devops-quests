v1.5.0 deepens incident practice across Kubernetes, Helm, OpenTofu, Ansible, GitOps and Vault: repairing the first visible symptom is no longer enough when the task also requires recovery, delivery to consumers or preserved data.

- 57/66 quests deepened (dq-82); 9 pending: 066, 075, 078, 122, 123, 030, 040, 060, 090 — shipped in previous form.
- Deepened quests add connected failures and recovery steps, checks of the running result and its consumers, and explicit conditions that must remain intact. Guides and hints follow the revised tasks. Examples include PVC data recovery, CPU and memory diagnosis, Helm release repair, OpenTofu state ownership and locking, Ansible delivery, GitOps handovers and Vault consumer adoption (dq-82).
- The quest briefing is simpler and shows your personal best directly when one exists. Bay controls are documented and announced at login; preserved conditions are separated from repair progress. The Basics loops quest now iterates over route data (dq-86; dq-82).
- Checks share quantity, selector, admission, Ansible, OpenTofu and workload validation helpers while retaining quest-specific behavior (dq-74).
- Local source builds use a separate mutable image namespace for each worktree, allowing checkouts to build without retagging each other's workstation and kind images (dq-27).

Known limits: dq-82 remains in progress. The nine quests listed above remain playable in their previous form; their deepening is planned for a later release.

No database schema migration is included. Accounts, progress, pending briefing hints and saved Lab files are preserved. Deployments refuse active attempts, pending briefing hints and active or incompatible saved Labs. The recorded previous release remains available for rollback through the documented operator procedure. Dependency snapshots are unchanged from v1.4.0.
