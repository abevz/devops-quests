# DevOps Quests v1.6.0

## Interview drill

All 134 existing interview questions are available as soon as you sign in, including 18 questions across nine theory topics. Browse by difficulty, topic and quest with question counts at each level. Search question text and key points in the current reading language, then narrow the results by topic or quest status. Each quest question shows whether you completed its quest. Use the completed-only filter to review questions from quests you have passed. The new controls are available in English, German, Spanish, Polish and Russian (dq-88).

## Hints and check feedback

- The revised hint ladders cover all 130 quests. Hints follow the stages of multi-stage incidents and point to the relevant section of a Guide or a short verification snippet (dq-14).
- Failed checks explain the specific unsatisfied conditions in the Bay and link to relevant Guide sections. When several failures are related, the feedback shows which prerequisite to address first and which checks to retry afterward. Independent failures stay visible. The original probe output remains available (dq-16).
- Guide lessons, localized examples and the authoring validator were updated alongside the hints and diagnostics. The validator checks that hint and diagnostic links resolve to their intended sections (dq-14, dq-16).

## Incident practice

The nine quests still awaiting deepening in v1.5.0 received new recovery scenarios and updated checks and reference solutions (dq-82).

- **030, 040, 060 and 090** now exercise release relay availability, controlled maintenance handovers, fleet release recovery and checkpoint recovery.
- **066, 075, 078, 122 and 123** deepen registry and webhook recovery, Argo CD application hierarchy and fleet delivery. The GitOps and recovery guidance follows the revised exercises.

## Installation and upgrades

There is no database schema migration. Accounts, progress, pending briefing hints and saved Lab files carry forward under the documented upgrade and rollback procedure. Sandbox image build inputs and the pinned CI dependency snapshots are unchanged from v1.5.0.
